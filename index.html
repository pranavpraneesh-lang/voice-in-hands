About the project

ENGINEERING FOR A MORE ACCESSIBLE CONVERSATION.

PROBLEM

Communication can be difficult between people who use sign language and people who do not understand it.

APPROACH

Voice In Hands uses wearable sensors to detect finger bending and hand movement. Sensor information is processed through an ESP32, which identifies selected predefined gestures and communicates the resulting information to a connected interface.

GOAL

Develop an affordable, portable prototype that can assist communication by converting selected hand gestures into readable text and audible speech.
